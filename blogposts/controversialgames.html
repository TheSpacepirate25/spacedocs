controversial games

Published June 12, 2026

A few days ago I was watching IGN's review of Mixtape, a Telltale Games style narrative / adventure game that got ridiculously high reviews by video game critics, but pretty horrible reviews by players. Most people described it as a walking simulator or a 'slightly interactive movie' rather than an actual game. It definitely has its target audience, but most 'gamers' would probably hate it.

Mixtape got me thinking about other games that other people like but I hate, or vice versa. So, here's a few of my controversial takes on games.

Terraria

Starting off with my most controversial take, I don't really like Terraria. It's a great game and I love the RPG elements, but the building is just so finicky. You have to be close by to build stuff, the layers are a bit confusing, and building large structures is really time consuming. Now, you could say all of these things about Minecraft, but I've been playing it for way longer and I'm more comfortable with building there. If I forced myself to play it until I got used to the building system, I might enjoy it. But I just don't really have the time, and I much prefer Minecraft mods.

Jedi: Fallen Order

I DESPISE the parkour and movement in this game. I'm playing on PC so it might be a lot better on controller, but I can never line up a jump properly or do any parkour without failing at least once. Again, it might be better on controller, but I still dislike it.

No Man's Sky

Now don't get me wrong, I really like this game. I've played it for over 50 hours. I love the developers and all the updates. But it's more of an exploration game rather than a space game. They're doing a lot to improve both the gameplay and progression, but it'll always be an exploration game.

Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts

Lots of people really hate this game and I don't get why. It's just a basic sniper / stealth game. It doesn't have to be particularly innovative or anything, it's just a good game. But loads of people think it's a Sniper Elite knockoff or another lame stealth game. It's a good game.

Minecraft Dungeons

Yes, it's a Diablo knockoff. Does that mean it's a bad game? No. It's still great in its own way. I've played both Diablo and Minecraft Dungeons and both have their strengths and weaknesses. Diablo has a great atmosphere, really cool spells, classes, and a pretty good equipment system. Dungeons doesn't use classes or spells, instead your class is defined by your weapon, so you can mix and match however you like, or swap playstyles when you like. Both are equally good games.

Hearts of Iron 4

I like Stellaris, Surviving Mars, Aquatico and Battle for Polytopia, all are great 4X or management games. But HOI4 is just too much for me. Drawing battle lines is kind of finicky, it's hard to see what my economy is like until it's in shambles, and overall I think Stellaris is a much friendlier game. Instead of having arbitrary units and resources in HOI4, in Stellaris you can see exactly how much stuff you make, where it's going, how it's being produced and your stockpiles of it. In my opinion, Stellaris is much better than HOI4, but to each their own.
