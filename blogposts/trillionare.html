So we've just got our first trillionare.

Personally, I think that much money shouldn't be in the hands of a rich white guy, so here's what I want spend a trillion dollars on as a significantly less rich white guy.

World Hunger

The UN predicted that it would cost $13 billion to assist 110 million vulnerable people. By that figure, it would cost about $40 billion to assist all people suffering from a critical crisis level of hunger. However, there's another statistic that the UN put out saying it would cost $93 billion annually (from 2025 to 2030) to have zero hunger by 2030, so about $500 billion. Famously, our now trillionare in question once asked how much it would cost to end world hunger. The UN replied and said it would be around $7 billion, and the WFP gave him a detailed plan on how $6.6 billion could feed 42 million highly vulnerable people facing famine, which is pretty close to the first figure. I'm going to ballpark solving world hunger at $300 billion, since there are a lot of different statistics floating around.

Debt

Australia's total household debt is about $3.4 trillion, but we have only about $43 billion in credit card debt, so we could pay off the credit card debt for every Australia. If you didn't know, Australia has a thing called HECS debt, which is basically where you take out a loan to pay for university, and you pay it back once you start making over a certain amount of money each year. The current outstanding HECS debt for everyone in Australia summed up is around $78 billion for 3 million Australians. The US is a LOT worse with around 1.7 trillion in student debt, $13.2 trillion in mortgages and $1.2 trillion in credit card debt. Even our trillionare doesn't have that much, so we'll just pay off HECS debt and credit card debt for Australians.

Space exploration

I really think space exploration isn't getting enough love right now. NASA's current budget is $24.4 billion, so let's quadruple it to $100 billion. Why not. Let's increase the ESA's budget too, from $9.5 billion to $95 billion, 10 times their current budget.

Fun Stuff

We still have $384 billion left, so I want to buy some fun stuff. There's a three-level 1600 square metre penthouse in Sydney that was purchased by Yan Zhang for $141 million, so let's buy him and his house out for $200 million. Actually, let's buy 5. One for me and four for my friends. Let's also bump up Greenpeace's budget from $435 million to $5 billion. I'm a big fan of not burning to death so this is important.

Now with my last roughly $379 billion, I want 55,571,428,571 McSmart meals. Rounding up from $6.95 to $7 for Ronald McDonald House, so $2,778,571,428 from 5 cent donations. So everyone in the world can have about 6 McSmart meals. Or I can have all 55 billion.

In conclusion, I can definitely be trusted with a trillion dollars. Mr Musk, my LinkedIn messages are open.
